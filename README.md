# 🩺 Public Health Data Science & Epidemiological Systems Portfolio

Welcome to my central health data analytics and epidemiological software engineering portfolio. This repository brings together production-grade interactive platforms, spatial disease surveillance systems, and clinical decision support tools targeting global health priorities across Africa and high-burden international regions.

---

## 🔬 Featured Projects

### 1. 🧪 [TB Sentinel | Global High-Burden TB Surveillance & Triage](https://github.com/chinwokeuba/tb-sentinel)
* **Domain:** Tuberculosis surveillance across 8 global high-burden nations.
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Streamlit, PyDeck, Plotly, Pandas.
* **Key Features:** Spatial risk matrix, multi-parameter clinical intake & WHO regimen prescribing engine (DS-TB, MDR-TB, BPaLM), live patient cohort registration, DOTS adherence monitoring, and regional drug supply chain forecasting.
* **Live App:** [TB Sentinel App](https://tb-sentinel.streamlit.app) *(Update with your live link)*

### 2. 🦟 [Malaria Risk Simulator & Environmental Surveillance System]
* **Domain:** Predictive environmental risk modeling and vector density simulation.
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Streamlit, Open-Meteo API, Plotly, Pandas.
* **Key Features:** Dynamic climate integration (rainfall, temperature, humidity), real-time infection risk calculations, regional risk mapping, and vector control resource planning.

### 3. 🎗️ [HIV Prevention Tracker & Clinical Cohort Analytics]
* **Domain:** HIV prevention tracking, PrEP adherence monitoring, and intervention planning.
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Streamlit, MySQL Workbench, Plotly, Pandas.
* **Key Features:** Risk scoring engines, cohort retention dashboards, and demographic resource allocation calculators.
